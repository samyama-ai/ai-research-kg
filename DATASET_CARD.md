# Dataset Card — AI Research KG

## Summary
A knowledge graph of 1,000 open problems in AI and machine learning: 29,696 nodes and 62,845 edges, built from the `ai_research` catalog. Sibling of the DBMS Research KG.

## Source

| Source | What it gives | Licence | Real or synthetic |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`github.com/samyama-ai/ai_research`](https://github.com/samyama-ai/ai_research) @ `a02e87b368016fd9568ba5ae8561ea601a8dee41` | every node and edge | **none stated** (the DBMS sibling states CC BY 4.0; see `docs/standards.md` §5) | **real** data, see note below |

That is the only source: no external APIs, no LLM extraction, no synthetic data.

**Note on "real".** Every node carries `provenance: "real"`, meaning it is parsed from the source and not generated by this repo. The catalog's own prose is marked `provenance: synthesized` in its frontmatter, which is kept on Problem nodes as `catalog_provenance`. All 1,000 problems are `synthesized` today, so the catalog's text has not been individually verified by its authors. Treat the graph as a map for orientation; cite the linked papers, not the graph.

## How each part is built

| Part | Rule | Method tag |
|---|---|---|
| Problem, Topic, Paper, Person, Venue, Assumption, OpenQuestion, FutureDirection | parsed from fixed sections of each Markdown file | `parsed` |
| Obstruction | "Why It Is Hard" mapped by keywords to the 5 obstruction types named in the catalog's `TEMPLATE.md` | `keyword-classified` |
| Institution | "Current Research" matched against a fixed list of 50 labs and universities (`etl/parse.py: INSTITUTIONS`) | `name-matched` |
| RELATED_TO | computed: two problems that cite ≥2 of the same papers, top 5 per problem by Jaccard score | edge `method: shared_papers` |
| CITES.role | ~500 free-text reference tags grouped into 7 roles; the original tag is kept in `role_raw` | — |

## Intended uses
- Exploring which problems, papers, people and labs connect across AI research topics.
- Finding research opportunities: empirically open problems, cheap next experiments, problems no lab is named on.
- A Samyama case study that mirrors the DBMS Research KG.

## Out of scope
- Benchmark leaderboards, model rankings, funding, citation counts from outside the catalog.
- Identity resolution of people (see limits).
- Being cited as a primary source.

## Known limits
- **People:** authors appear in three forms (surname / initials / full name) and are not merged, so a bare surname like "Zhang" stands for many people. Use `name_form = 'full'` for people questions.
- **Methods:** not extracted in v1.
- **Institutions:** only the 50 names in the list are found; others are missed.
- **Experiment budgets:** `compute_budget_gpu_hours` is parsed for 361 of 1,000 experiments, from text like "~200 A100-hours"; the rest are empty, never guessed.
- **One source file** has sections 9 and 10 swapped; the parser finds sections by name, so it's handled.
