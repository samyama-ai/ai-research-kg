## 1. Paper studied

### Knowledge Graphs as the Missing Data Layer for LLM-Based Industrial Asset Operations

M. Mandarapu and S. Kunkunuru, [arXiv:2605.26874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26874) (cs.DB; v3 2026-06-29); a version appears in the VLDB 2026 AgentGraph workshop.

The paper investigates how structured knowledge graphs can provide a grounding layer for LLM-based systems operating over industrial asset information.

The main idea I took from the paper is that an LLM's performance depends strongly on the information available to it. A structured knowledge graph can provide explicit, queryable and grounded information that is difficult for an LLM to reliably recover from unstructured documents alone.

The paper compares different ways of providing information to an LLM and shows that graph-based grounding can substantially improve performance, particularly when the graph contains information that is not already part of the model's parametric knowledge.

This was relevant to the AI Research KG work because the same general idea applies to research information: instead of keeping research problems, topics, papers and relationships only as unstructured text, they can be represented as structured graph entities and relationships that can be queried directly.

## 2. AI Research Knowledge Graph — v1

Built the first working version of the AI Research Knowledge Graph.

The KG organizes AI research information around:

- Research problems (1,000, one per catalog page) and the 35 topics they sit in
- Research papers they cite, with the citation's role
- Authors as written in the references (not resolved to people: a bare surname stays a bare surname)
- Institutions named in "Current Research", matched against a fixed list of 51 names
- The problem's own assumptions, open questions, obstructions and proposed next experiment
- A computed link between problems that cite the same papers

The goal is to make AI research information queryable as a graph and provide a structured foundation for answering questions about research problems, topics, papers and people.

## 3. Implementation completed

### Repository and project setup

Set up the `ai-research-kg` repository and configured the project environment.

The repository contains the core KG implementation, ETL pipeline, schema documentation and benchmark/query code.

### Schema

Implemented the initial graph schema defining the entities and relationships required for the current research scope.

The schema is documented in:

```text
docs/schema.md
```

### ETL / parsing

Implemented the parser that turns each catalog page into nodes and edges, section by section: frontmatter → Problem; "Key References" → Paper, Person, Venue and CITES/AUTHORED_BY/APPEARED_AT; "Formal Setting" → Assumption; "What Is Not Known" → OpenQuestion; "Why It Is Hard" → Obstruction (keyword-mapped to the catalog's five types); "Concrete Next Experiment" → FutureDirection; "Current Research" → Institution and ACTIVE_ON. Sections are found by heading text, not number. Papers are deduplicated by arXiv id, then title. RELATED_TO is computed from shared cited papers.

Main implementation (on 2026-10-07 one file; split on 2026-10-12 into `etl/rules.py`, `etl/text.py`, `etl/parse.py`, `etl/build.py` after review):

```text

```

### Graph loading

Implemented the graph loading pipeline:

```text
etl/loader.py
```

The loader loads the generated nodes and relationships into the graph and performs count validation.

## 6. Standards / interoperability work

Started the standards work (`docs/standards.md`, v0.2). What v1 actually uses: the catalog's own taxonomy (35 topics), arXiv identifiers as paper ids, ISO 8601 year-months, and PROV-O's field names as a pattern (`source`, `source_url`, `method`). Everything else in that document is *recorded as planned or rejected*, not adopted: ORCID, DBLP, OpenAlex and ROR need external lookups that v1 does not make; ACM CCS and Semantic Scholar are deliberately not used; NIST AI RMF, the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001 and the OECD principles are for a later Responsible AI extension.
