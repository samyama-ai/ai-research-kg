## 1. Paper studied

### Knowledge Graphs as the Missing Data Layer for LLM-Based Industrial Asset Operations

The paper investigates how structured knowledge graphs can provide a grounding layer for LLM-based systems operating over industrial asset information.

The main idea I took from the paper is that an LLM's performance depends strongly on the information available to it. A structured knowledge graph can provide explicit, queryable and grounded information that is difficult for an LLM to reliably recover from unstructured documents alone.

The paper compares different ways of providing information to an LLM and shows that graph-based grounding can substantially improve performance, particularly when the graph contains information that is not already part of the model's parametric knowledge.

This was relevant to the AI Research KG work because the same general idea applies to research information: instead of keeping research problems, topics, papers and relationships only as unstructured text, they can be represented as structured graph entities and relationships that can be queried directly.

## 2. AI Research Knowledge Graph — v1

Built the first working version of the AI Research Knowledge Graph.

The KG is designed to organize AI research information around:

- Research problems
- AI research topics
- Research papers
- Researchers
- Institutions
- Relationships between these entities

The goal is to make AI research information queryable as a graph and provide a structured foundation for answering questions about research problems, topics, papers and people.

Implemented the parsing pipeline that converts the source AI research data into graph entities and relationships.

Main implementation:

```text

```

The parser converts the source problem/topic information into the structured representation required by the graph.

### Graph loading

Implemented the graph loading pipeline:

```text
etl/loader.py
```

The loader loads the generated nodes and relationships into the graph and performs count validation.
